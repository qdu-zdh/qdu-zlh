研究思路与总体方法论 Research Philosophy & Methodology

我的研究聚焦复杂群体系统的演化机理与协同操控，严格遵循‘现象解析、协议设计、场景拓展、方法革新、仿生观测’的学术逻辑。研究从系统底层性能分析起步，逐步进入控制协议设计与复杂编队实现；针对大规模系统的维度灾难，引入平均场博弈（MFG）机制，实现了从‘精准控制’向‘群体柔性引导’的范式跨越。为摆脱当前群体研究中过度集中于启发式智能优化算法所导致的底层机理融合匮乏，我开发了多款针对生物、物理及社会群体模型的仿真工具，将实证观察作为理论校验的基石。在方法革新层面，我将机器学习深度融入李雅普诺夫稳定性分析与目标规划控制中，推动动力学研究与智能学习的交叉融合。除此之外，将重点关注两大方向：一是‘从人工构造规则到数据学习涌现’的多智能体强化学习（MARL）；二是探索将机器学习全流程介入稳定性保证、目标规划与运动控制的具身智能范式。整体研究致力于深入探索群体行为背后深层的动力学本源与驱动机制。

My research focuses on the evolutionary mechanisms and collaborative manipulation of complex swarm systems, strictly adhering to the academic logic of 'phenomenon analysis, protocol design, scenario expansion, methodological innovation, and biomimetic observation'. The research begins with the analysis of the system's underlying performance, gradually delving into control protocol design and complex formation implementation. To address the dimensionality curse of large-scale systems, I introduce the mean-field game (MFG) mechanism, achieving a paradigm shift from 'precise control' to 'flexible swarm guidance'. To overcome the lack of underlying mechanisms caused by the over-reliance on heuristic intelligent optimization algorithms in current swarm research, I have developed several simulation tools for biological, physical, and social swarm models, using empirical observation as the cornerstone of theoretical verification. In terms of methodological innovation, I deeply integrate machine learning into Lyapunov stability analysis and goal-oriented control, promoting the cross-fertilization of dynamics research and intelligent learning. In the future, I will focus on two main directions: first, researching multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) 'from artificially constructed rules to data-driven emergence'; and second, exploring embodied intelligence paradigms that integrate machine learning throughout the entire process of stability assurance, goal-oriented planning, and motion control. The overall research is dedicated to exploring the underlying dynamics and driving mechanisms behind group behavior.

复杂网络性能分析方法 Complex Network Performance Analysis Methods

在多智能体系统一致性控制的研究中，核心要务是对其动态机制与集体性能进行统一的理论分析。从系统调控的视角出发，网络的可控性与可观性是实现任何动态网络综合设计的先决条件。具体而言，特定的网络拓扑结构往往蕴含着深层的代数与几何性质，这些性质（如等价划分）能够极大助力对系统不可控或不可观模式的快速识别与降维分析。

尽管等价划分等工具在复杂网络性能评估中起到了关键的判别作用，但早期的工作往往基于极其理想化的标量网络条件提出。随着现代协同系统向多维状态同步演进，网络交互呈现出多维耦合、符号对抗以及高阶拓扑等复杂特征，传统定义与被动分析方法的有效性面临着严峻的理论挑战。

为了克服这些局限，实现从被动边界评估向主动的、多项式时间内的网络综合设计的跨越，本部分工作围绕复杂网络的可控性进行了系统性的理论拓展，具体涵盖以下研究维度：符号与离散网络的可控性分析： 突破了传统非负标量权重的限制，深入研究了符号网络架构下扩散耦合系统，以及一般线性离散多智能体系统的可控性与可观性问题（文献 [1], [2]）。矩阵权重网络的性能评估与强结构可控性（SSC）： 针对多维状态空间内的交叉维度耦合特性，全面探讨了矩阵权重符号网络的可控性，并系统提出了强结构可控子空间的维度估计方法与挤压边界工具（文献 [3], [4], [5], [7]）。多项式时间的主动领导者合成： 将分析范式从被动的结构评估转移到主动的网络设计，在矩阵权重网络中提出了针对强结构可控性的领导者选择与主动合成算法，有效解决了底层的组合优化难题（文献 [6], [7]）。高阶拓扑结构的建模与扩展： 将矩阵权重网络的能控性理论进一步推广至超图（Hypergraphs）及超-超图架构，探索了更为复杂的高阶群体交互建模及其控制性能分析（文献 [8]）。

对于群体系统，我们首先注意到的是其客观现象和性能，这一部分工作所从事的即性能分析方法的讨论和拓展。从调控的视角来看，我们所关心的性能在于其可控性和可观性，具体来说，特定的网络结构往往带来特定的性质，这些性质又进一步助力对系统性能的快速判断，等价划分、距离划分、零迫集等图论工具即是此类。

For swarm systems, our primary focus is their objective phenomena and performance. This section discusses and expands upon performance analysis methods. From a regulatory perspective, we are concerned with controllability and observability. Specifically, certain network structures induce particular properties, which further facilitate rapid assessment of system performance (e.g., equitable partitions).

The uncontrollable and unobservable properties brought by equitable partitions act as quick diagnostics in complex network analysis. However, prior works often relied on highly idealized network conditions, leaving the validity of definitions and analysis methods under realistic settings unknown. To transition from passive performance boundary evaluation to active, polynomial-time leader synthesis[cite: 1], we conducted the following research:

We systematically explored scenarios including time delays, heterogeneity, switching topologies, matrix weights, general linear systems, structural controllability, strong structural controllability, and optimal leader selection[cite: 1, 2]. We proposed novel definitions of equitable partitions (e.g., Layer-specific Distance Partition)[cite: 2], and mathematically isolated uncontrollability roots to topological symmetry equivalence and dimension-specific isolation[cite: 1]. By introducing symmetry-breaking algorithms like multi-layer Weisfeiler-Lehman refinement, GWLS, SBM, and PEM, we successfully bypassed NP-hard combinatorial bottlenecks[cite: 1, 2]. The systematic integration of these six works provides a comprehensive reference for adapting basic graph-theoretic tools to both analyze and proactively design complex network scenarios.

多智能体系统控制基本理论 Fundamental Theory of Multi-Agent System Control

拥有了系统性能分析做基础,我们进一步关注其调控问题，抽象成多智能体问题来说，即一般系统的控制协议设计问题。

进一步又可以分成几个子问题，一、不同类型的控制问题如何设计协议。二、所设计的协议是否一定可以实现目标（总是可以求出可用的参数，而不是求出参数才可以用）。三是相比于其他协议有什么好处，我们开展了如下研究：

提出了一致性、部分一致性、输出一致性的统一分析框架、提出了系统可一致性新的分析方法，与上述性能分析方法实现了配套，从使用信息的视角提出了多种新型控制协议。

Building upon system performance analysis, we focus on their regulation—abstracted into multi-agent problems, this translates to general control protocol design.

This subdivides into: 1. How to design protocols for various control paradigms. 2. Whether the designed protocols theoretically guarantee the objective (ensuring parameters *can* be found fundamentally, rather than only working *if* they happen to be found). 3. Their theoretical advantages over existing protocols.

We proposed a unified analysis framework for consensus, partial state consensus, and output consensus; introduced new consensus analysis methods complementing the aforementioned performance analyses; and designed various novel control protocols from an information-utilization perspective.

Researchers have realized some problems exceed traditional mathematical methods. Finding new driving strategies, auxiliary stability judgments, and data-driven manipulation methods is a hot topic and a grand puzzle for the post-2025 era.

生物和社会启发 Bio-Inspiration

自然界（包括人类社区）是目前人类所发现的最精密的群体系统。进行群体分析与控制时，近年来的一个趋势是效仿生物界和社会学界，若脱离实证观察，极易陷入缺乏依据的“自我想象”。即这个方法即使有效，我们也不能确定该机制是否真的存在。

Nature (including human communities) is the most precise swarm system discovered by humanity so far. When conducting swarm analysis and control, a recent trend is to emulate the biological and sociological worlds. Without empirical observation, it's easy to fall into baseless "self-imagination". That is, even if a method works, we cannot be certain the mechanism actually exists.

需要注意的是，近年来出现了很多讨论生物集群控制、观点动力学调控的工作，相比于使用控制手段调控这些方程，我们更倾向于验证控制手段的可行性，这是一个艰苦的交叉工作，留待后续开展。

It should be noted that many recent works discuss biological swarm control and opinion dynamics regulation. Rather than simply using control methods to regulate these equations, we prefer to verify the feasibility of the control means. This is arduous interdisciplinary work, left for future pursuit.

Doing research is like searching for the optimal solution in a high-dimensional unknown space; you will inevitably fall into local minima, facing the tedium of derivations and code errors. Meanwhile, the new technologies emerging every moment might make you hesitate, feel frustrated, or overwhelmed.

This requires the patience to endure obscurity and rigorous mathematical logic. But beyond that, I hope you are a "vivid" and "alive" person. Technology should serve the problem, not the other way around.

When your research hits a bottleneck, try reading some history, playing a game, or traveling. "How can you have a worldview if you haven't viewed the world?" A scholar with a broad vision and profound humanistic background can often see the inspiration and vitality in complex systems that others cannot.
